## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
```text
Tapwing is a fast, clean-room Android keyboard (English QWERTY & German QWERTZ) crafted around expressive capsule keys, privacy-first on-device intelligence, and zero ads or telemetry.

KEY FEATURES

• Crisp Capsule Layout & Optional Translucent Glass Mode
Clean keys with a dedicated 1–0 number row, centered secondary symbols, continuous live height resizing (75%–135%), plus an optional Liquid Glass visual style with spring keycap animations.

• Fully Customizable Top Quick-Actions Bar
Choose which shortcuts sit above your keyboard and reorder them anytime—either directly via long-press on the keyboard strip or in the minimalist settings editor.

• Personal Dictionary (.zip Import & Export) & Adaptive Learning
Import personal dictionaries seamlessly via .zip (tab-separated dictionary.txt), .txt, .csv/.tsv, or .json and export backups anytime. Tapwing learns your vocabulary and next-word pairs locally and remembers when you undo an autocorrection.

• Built-In Productivity Power Tools
- Cursor & Text-Edit D-pad (Select, Copy, Cut, Paste)
- Inline Calculator right while typing (e.g., 24*7= → 168)
- Text shortcuts plus dynamic /date and /time expansions
- Swipe backspace word delete with 1-tap Proactive Undo
- One-handed mode (left/right) & temporary Incognito mode

• Natural Voice Dictation Cleanup
Delegates speech input to Android's SpeechRecognizer and removes filler words, self-corrections, and spoken punctuation locally on your device, complete with a minimizable voice toolbar and rotation grace window.

• AI Writing Tools (Offline + Optional Bring-Your-Own-Key)
Proofread, rephrase, or adjust tone (Professional, Casual, Concise, Emotive). Works offline out of the box using built-in rules, or optionally connects using your own API key encrypted in hardware-backed Android Keystore.

• Extended Clipboard & On-Device OCR
Store text clips with configurable retention (1 hour up to Never), scan printed text with the live CameraX + ML Kit viewfinder, or extract text from single selected photos via Android's System Photo Picker—without broad gallery permissions.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Typing, dictionary learning, clipboard history, and camera OCR stay locally on your device. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no hidden uploads.
```
